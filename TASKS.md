## Phase 8 — Organisation, hierarchy and access control

This phase is the base for everything after it. It replaces "one role + is_admin" with an
org chart: **CEO → Managers → Team Leads → Members**, grouped into departments and teams.

### 8.1 Org structure
- [x] **8.1.1** `departments` table: Sales, BDT, Operations, HR (admin-editable list)
      Exists since Phase 10 (HR settings). Seed adds the four; teams now belong to one (migration 00012).
- [x] **8.1.2** `teams` table: each belongs to one department and has a name, a Team Lead and members.
      Operations gets several teams (e.g. Shopify, Web Dev, Design, SEO, Content).
      A **team owns many projects; every project belongs to exactly one team** (`projects.team_id`).
- [~] **8.1.3** `team_members` with a primary team per user (multi-team membership: see Q3)
      Done for Ops members: one team each (migration 00011). Teams page `/admin/teams` for HR + CEO. Multi-team waits on Q3.
- [x] **8.1.4** `users.manager_id` (reporting line) and `users.department_id`
      Kept on the HR record (`employees.manager_id`, `employees.department_id`) since Phase 10.
- [x] **8.1.5** Org-chart page: departments, then teams, then people, with the reporting line
      For the CEO, HR and Team Leaders only; everyone else sees "You report to …" on their dashboard.

### 8.0 Roles groundwork (done early, 2026-10-18)
- [x] **8.0.1** Management → **CEO** (one active CEO, DB-enforced; hand over with `opsctl transfer-ceo`). Still never edits projects.
- [x] **8.0.2** New **HR** role: accounts, roles, HR records, attendance/leave for everyone, HR settings; no project access.
- [x] **8.0.3** Only HR and the CEO change roles and accounts. Only the CEO grants/removes HR and admin access or touches the CEO account. Nobody changes their own access.
- [x] **8.0.4** Admin flag narrowed to system setup (profiles, project types, app settings); the CEO can also do it.

### 8.2 Hierarchy roles
**Decision 2026-10-18 (Q4): no Managers for now.** The hierarchy stays CEO → Team Leaders → Members (plus Sales and HR); 8.2.1–8.2.3 are on hold until a Manager level is wanted. 8.2.4 is done (8.0.1).
- [ ] **8.2.1** New levels: `ceo` (exactly one, super admin), `manager`, `team_lead`, `member`.
      Each user also gets a department/function (Sales, BDT, Ops, HR).
- [ ] **8.2.2** Migrate existing users: `tl` becomes an Ops team_lead, `member` an Ops member,
      `sales` a Sales member, `management` a manager or the CEO (Q1). `is_admin` is replaced by the CEO role and scoped permissions.
- [ ] **8.2.3** Rewrite `internal/policy` as level + department + team scope, with a full test matrix
- [ ] **8.2.4** Only one CEO, enforced by the DB (partial unique index). CEO handover is CLI-only (`opsctl transfer-ceo`).
- [x] **8.2.5** **Department heads** (Q13, 2026-10-23): `departments.head_id` (migration 00020). The head is an active Team Leader who runs every team in the department with a lead's powers: sees, assigns, approves and completes those teams' projects, is notified about them, and their workload covers all of them. One person may head several departments. Set under HR settings → Departments (HR + CEO); shown on the org chart. A head must hand the seat over before a role change or deactivation. Not a new role: the Manager level in 8.2.1 stays parked.

### 8.3 Access management ("delete access")
**Decision 2026-10-18 (Q2): only HR and the CEO change or revoke access** (already enforced, 8.0.3). The Manager / Team Lead rows below are dropped.

- [ ] **8.3.5** Users page scoped to what each actor can manage, with invite, edit, revoke and restore

## Phase 9 — BDT (Business Development Team) and self orders

> [!] **On hold.** Self orders with pre-written buyer/seller conversations are staged transactions meant to look real to the marketplace. Claude won't build this module. Pending a decision on the replacement scope (see Q10).

A self order is an order the company places **on its own marketplace profiles**, acting as the
client, to keep a profile active. BDT raises it, Sales handles it on the profile side, and the two
coordinate on the order. Self orders are **internal records**. They never enter the Ops project
flow, and they are kept out of real revenue and sales reports. *(Flow assumed; see Q5.)*
